Welcome to ScribeTrace

A calm companion to Zotero and Obsidian that preserves the verifiable history of your reading marks in open local files.

1

Switch on Zotero local access

ScribeTrace reads your existing PDF highlights and Markdown notes directly from Zotero running on this computer (127.0.0.1:23119). It never connects to the internet and never modifies your Zotero database.

In Zotero: Settings (or Preferences) → Advanced
☑ Allow other applications on this computer to communicate with Zotero
2

Choose your project folder

Select where your research notes and papers live. ScribeTrace creates a single local .scribetrace/ folder to store all history files.

3

Connect & verify

Press Connect to test local access. ScribeTrace queries Zotero only when you click; there is zero background polling or tracking.
